# Career Compass - Dataset Feature Specification

## Project Objective

Career Compass is an AI-powered Student Placement Intelligence and Career Readiness Platform designed to analyze student profiles, predict placement outcomes, assess career readiness, identify skill gaps, and provide personalized improvement recommendations.

---

The final Career Compass system will provide:
